Malware and Cyber Threats

WannaCry Ransomware Attack

The WannaCry ransomware attack occured on May 12, 2017, and affected roughly 200,000 computers across 150 countries. The attack targeted computers running Microsoft Windows by encrypting data and files and demanding ransom payments to unencrypt them. It famously affected the UK's National Health Service, causing disruptions in healthcare services, and was significant in its effects, but also displayed the need to update and secure devices against malware.
