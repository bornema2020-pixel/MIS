In the process of creating this website, I have learned a lot about management information systems, artificial intelligence, and cyber security. First and foremost, artificial intelligence encompasses so much of our day to day. It has become something people have begun to depend on, and while incredibly beneficial to studnets, businesses, and those in their daily lives, it can be potentially harmful. This goes into what I learned about cyber security. As beneficial as AI is, it knows the soft spots of our systems and softwares that can be exploited by several people. It is a tool to be used responsibly, but when used in malicious ways, it is incredibly important to stay on top of updating software, having firewalls and multi-factor authentication, and ensuring you have intricate passwords. This is incredibly important because management information systems must have good security and measures to protect the loads of information and data stored. These systems are fantastic are story and organizing data, but that information needs protected.
